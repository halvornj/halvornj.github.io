## a planning tool for students, to help choose courses and study groups.

_**this is a work in progress**_

This site is available at [emneplanleggeren.no](https://www.emneplanleggeren.no){:target="\_blank"}, and the source is available [here](https://github.com/halvornj/Emneplanleggeren){:target="\_blank}.
This site was made in collaboration with [Sander Rasmussen](https://sanderrasmussen.com/){:target="\_blank"}.

### what it does

A centralized, easy to use, website for viewing courses and study groups at UiO. The site is made to help students plan their semester, and to help them choose courses and study groups. The site allows anyone to select courses to view in the calendar window, and what study groups for those courses to show in the calendar. This gives the user a calendar view of the lectures, workshops and group lectures they have selected, and makes it easy to see when other groups for the same course have their lectures.

### why it does it

For students at the University of Oslo, at least the ones I know at the Institute of Informatics, it is a hassle to plan your semester. You see, you typically have to sign up to a group lecture for each course. Actually, you have to select _three_ groups, and arrange them according to which one you like the most. The problem is that with typically 3 courses, that's 9 groups you have to make sure don't collide with any lecture for any course, or any workshop. This becomes even more of a hassle when you have free choice of subjects, because now you have to make sure that the lectures for the courses you want to take don't collide with each other. This is a problem that can be solved with a calendar, and that's what this site does.  
This is of course also information that you could easily obtain by searching the relevant course pages. But then, you'd have to waste time finding it, **and crucially**, keep the timeslots for 10-15 different events in your head while planning. Seeing as _keeping multiple different events organized by time_ is what calendars are made for, we made the calendar.

### what will be done

As teh top of this page says, [emneplanleggeren.no](https://www.emneplanleggeren.no){:target="\_blank"} is a work in progress. At the time of writing, the MVP is done, and the site is live. The next steps are currently:

- add more courses, expand beyond the Institute of Informatics
- add more universities. NTNU may be the next one on the list, but we would have to do some research to see if this is even something they need.
- UI upgrades. The site could do with some more advanced UI features, the current plan is to do some interviews/surveys to see what the users want.
